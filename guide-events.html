How to List on Connect Nigeria

Choose a category, then follow the same seven core steps. Only the category, action and form stages change.

Estimated time 5 minutes

What are you listing?

Automobiles 4 stages: List your car now Deals 4 stages: List your deal now Events 5 stages: Go round in events now Jobs 3 stages: List your job opening now Real Estate 4 stages: List your property now

In this guide

  • 1 Visit the Connect Nigeria site
  • 2 Log in or sign up
  • 3 Click on "List Now"
  • 4 Select the "Events" Category
  • 5 Provide Requested Details
  • 6 Review Your Details
  • 7 Submit Your Listing
5

Events has 5 form stages

General Info, Location & Special guest, Pricing, Photos, Review

STEP 1 OF 7

Visit the Connect Nigeria site

Go to www.connectnigeria.com

STEP 2 OF 7

Log in or sign up

Click "Sign Up" if you haven't registered on the Connect Nigeria platform. If you already have an account, click "Sign In", input your email address and password, then click "Log in"

STEP 3 OF 7

Click on "List Now"

Click on the "List Now" button at the top right corner of the page. This will take you to the "Select a Category" page.

STEP 4 OF 7

Select the "Events" Category

Click on the "Events" category on the page. A disclaimer popup will appear. Read the disclaimer and click on the "Proceed" button at the bottom of the popup.

STEP 5 OF 7

Provide Requested Details

Fill in the required details, upload required images, and click on "Next Step". Do this for each stage that follows. There are four stages in total: General Info, Location and Features, Photos, and Review.

STEP 6 OF 7

Review Your Details

Review the details you have provided to ascertain that they are correct.

STEP 7 OF 7

Submit Your Listing

Click on "Submit" to submit your listing for publication.
