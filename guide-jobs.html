Stage 01 • Search

Filter by Experience, Location & Salary

Search verified job boards on ConnectNigeria. Focus on postings tagged with "Verified Employer" to avoid ghost recruitment postings and unauthorized agents.

Stage 02 • Profile

Complete ConnectNigeria Professional Badge

A complete profile with verified portfolio links, educational certifications, and peer endorsements receives up to 4x more direct outreach from corporate hiring directors.

https://connectnigeria.com/profile/talent-preview
Profile Status: 100% Complete • Verified Talent Badge Active
Stage 03 • Application

Craft a Customized Cover Proposal

Address the hiring team directly. Highlight concrete quantitative achievements (e.g. "Grew monthly active users by 35% across Nigerian cities") rather than generic descriptions.

https://connectnigeria.com/jobs/apply/fintech-lead
Application: Submitted to HR Director at PaySwitch
Stage 04 • Compensation

Benchmark Nigerian Salary & Perks Matrix

Review ConnectNigeria's annual Salary Survey for your job title. Account for current inflation indices, health insurance coverage, remote work allowances, and pension remittance (PFA).

https://connectnigeria.com/careers/salary-survey
Benchmark: 75th Percentile for Tech Leads in Lagos
Stage 05 • Interview

Technical Test & Panel Interview Preparation

Structure your responses using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Prepare questions about the team's engineering culture and roadmap in the African market.

https://connectnigeria.com/interviews/schedule/live
Interview Confirmed: Google Meet with Chief Product Officer
Stage 06 • Vetting

Complete Reference & Identity Vetting

Provide two senior professional referees from your previous employers. Verified references expedite the issuance of unconditional offer letters.

https://connectnigeria.com/vetting/status
Vetting Cleared: References and Identity Verified 100%
Stage 07 • Offer Letter

Review Terms, Sign & Start Onboarding

Examine probation period (usually 3–6 months in Nigeria), notice period, intellectual property clauses, and sign electronically through ConnectNigeria's legal contract portal.

https://connectnigeria.com/contracts/sign-offer
Offer Accepted: Congratulations on your new executive role!
