Stage 01 • Criteria

Define Location, Property Type & Total Cost

Account for all hidden fees in Nigerian property transactions: Agency fee (typically 10%), Legal fee (5–10%), Caution deposit (for rentals), and service charge maintenance fees.

Stage 02 • Agent Vetting

Verify Realtor Registration & License

Only transact with agents registered with the Lagos State Real Estate Regulatory Authority (LASRERA) or NIESV. Never pay inspection fees to freelance agents without a confirmed office location.

https://connectnigeria.com/realtors/verified-license
LASRERA Verified: Prestige Homes & Properties Ltd. ✓
Stage 03 • Virtual Tour

Filter Verified Listings with Video Walkthroughs

Avoid duplicate and outdated property photos. ConnectNigeria requires confirmed listings to feature uncut video walkthroughs showcasing the street access, drainage, and interior finish.

https://connectnigeria.com/property/virtual-tour/lk-902
Virtual Tour: 4K Drone Aerial & Interior Walkthrough Verified
Stage 04 • Physical Inspection

Conduct In-Person Structural & Flood Inspection

Inspect during daytime or immediately after rainfall to evaluate natural drainage. Bring a certified surveyor to test water pressure, electrical wiring, and check walls for dampness/cracks.

https://connectnigeria.com/inspection/book-surveyor
Inspection: Passed Structural & Plumbing Integrity Test
Stage 05 • Title Search

Conduct Legal Title Search at Lands Bureau

The most critical step in Nigeria. Your legal counsel must perform a search at the state Lands Registry (e.g. Alausa, Ikeja for Lagos) to confirm Certificate of Occupancy (C of O), Governor's Consent, or Gazette.

https://connectnigeria.com/legal/title-search-registry
Title Verified: Governor's Consent Registered (Vol 42, Page 12)
Stage 06 • Documentation

Draft Deed of Assignment or Tenancy Contract

Never sign a handwritten receipt. Have a certified Nigerian property lawyer draft the formal Deed of Assignment (for sales) or Tenancy Agreement with explicit clause for annual rent review and refund terms.

https://connectnigeria.com/documents/deed-of-assignment
Legal Documents: Deed of Assignment Formally Executed
Stage 07 • Handover

Release Funds via Escrow & Hand Over Keys

Final payout is disbursed through ConnectNigeria Escrow only after the original title deeds, keys, and estate clearance access cards are physically verified in your hands.
